# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Eclipse Community Code of Conduct](https://www.eclipse.org/org/documents/Community_Code_of_Conduct.php).

In short, we commit to making participation in this project a harassment-free, welcoming and inclusive experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests and discussions.
- Welcome newcomers and be patient with questions.
- Accept feedback gracefully and focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, insults or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without their permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

To report a violation, contact the project maintainers at [contact@tamboai.co](mailto:contact@tamboai.co), or the Eclipse Foundation at [codeofconduct@eclipse.org](mailto:codeofconduct@eclipse.org).
